# Support

## Reporting a bug

[Open an issue](https://github.com/twilightventures13/lens/issues/new/choose) and
pick the bug template. Name the plugin and its version (Settings > Plugins) plus
the IDE build (Help > About).

What helps most:

- the shape of the file that triggered it: rough size, format, compression, and a
  sample line if you can share one
- what you expected vs what happened
- the stack trace from Help > Show Log in Files, if there was an exception

Issues are checked daily. Fixes ship through regular plugin updates on the
marketplace listings. If a bug is blocking your work, say so in the issue and it
moves up the queue.

## Licensing

Trials, purchases and refunds are processed by JetBrains, so account and billing
questions go through your JetBrains account. Questions about what Free includes,
what Pro adds, or whether something is working as intended belong here in the
issues.
